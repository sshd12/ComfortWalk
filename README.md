# ComfortWalk

ComfortWalk is a navigation app I am currently developing after moving to San Francisco. I became interested in how different people may prefer different routes depending on their needs. For example, a commuter may prioritize travel time, while someone traveling with young children may prefer to avoid steep hills.

I am currently developing and testing the app while learning more about navigation and route selection.

## Current Focus
- User preferences
- Route recommendations
- Accessibility and comfort factors
- Testing different approaches to navigation

**Status:** Work in progress
